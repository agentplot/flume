flume's own steps are built: it runs on one host, from a launch to the
landing and the tidy. Each step ended with something running on real
work before the next one depended on it, and a step was done when a
check passed or an observation held. The step names are the plan's, in
agentplot/blueprints' `design/capture/plan.md`.

## flume's own steps

<Steps>

1. **L1 · The statement.** flume's statement is written from the capture
   design and the rules for teams and plans it takes over. It covers a
   launch, places, kinds and stages, prompt delivery, stage ends, status,
   tidying, and what flume never does. It is ruled, and amended as each
   new question is ruled.

2. **L2 · The repository's rules.** This repository, in Rust, holds its
   founding decisions: one place, one agent per stage; a stage ends on
   its deliverable; plugins outside the core. With them come its rules,
   its sluice gate, CI on Linux and macOS, releases from `cliff.toml`,
   and its OpenSpec configuration. sluice protects `main`, and every
   change lands through it.

3. **L3 · The core and the Claude Code plugin.** `flume launch`,
   `status`, `prompt`, `tell`, `hold`, `retry`, `stop`, `finish`,
   `drop`, `done`, `priority`, `tidy`, `settle`, `wait`, `inbox` and
   `wake`, with places, flume's fix, chore and change and a
   repository's or a launch's own kinds. A kind runs through on its
   own, a failed submission sends the work back, and the host keeps one
   queue by its callers' priorities. The agent's own hooks ack its start
   and its prompts, and stage ends are read from deliverables, a
   change's from its OpenSpec files. The plugin brings `/launch` and
   serves `status` over MCP. A fix and a change launched from a session
   land through sluice and their places are tidied, by the walkthroughs
   the gate runs.

4. **L4 · The herdr plugin.** A watch that settles every place as its
   agent changes state, the sidebar's rows and the popup of places in
   flight, with keys to open, tell, stop and retry. It installs from
   flume's release tag.

</Steps>

## Where it meets the others

<Steps>

1. **P6 · millpond sends an item through flume.** millpond's `send`
   names a repository and a kind, records the send, hands flume the
   brief, and writes the landing against the item from sluice's record.
   An inbox whose settings hand items to crew refuses a send.

   _Needs_ millpond's tools (P4) and L3. _Done when_ an item of two
   bumps sent from herdr lands through sluice, the item shows its
   landing, and a send from crew's inbox is refused.

2. **C7 · crew calls flume.** crew starts each stage's agent in a place
   through flume and reads stage ends from `flume status`, keeping its
   plans, bolts, slots, dispatcher, conductors and approvals.

   _Needs_ crew's amended record (C1) and L3. _Done when_ a bolt of one
   unit and one chore lands with every place and stage started through
   flume, and no crew code makes a worktree itself.

</Steps>

Others wait on flume too. headwater's plugins (H7) need L3 for the fix
key. sounding's core (Q3) needs L3 and its herdr plugin (Q4) needs L4,
since a sounding's place and session are flume's.
