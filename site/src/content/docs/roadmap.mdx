Nothing of flume is built yet. Each step ends with something running on
real work before the next one depends on it. A step is done when a
check passes or an observation holds. The step names are the plan's, in
agentplot/blueprints' `design/capture/plan.md`. flume needs only crew's
rules to extract from, so its own steps proceed beside millpond's.

## flume's own steps

<Steps>

1. **L1 · The statement.** flume's statement is written from the capture
   design and the crew rules it takes over: a launch, places, kinds and
   stages, prompt delivery, stage ends, status, tidying, and what flume
   never does. A fresh model reconstructs flume from it, and the
   questions it asks are ruled and folded back in.

   _Needs_ nothing. _Done when_ the reconstruction's questions are
   ruled and folded in, Vale is clean, and the statement lands on
   blueprints' `main`.

2. **L2 · The repository's rules.** This repository, in Rust on
   kit-standard's code profile, gains its founding decisions: one place,
   one agent per stage; a stage ends on its deliverable; plugins outside
   the core. With them come its rules, its sluice gate, `cliff.toml`
   and its OpenSpec configuration.

   _Needs_ L1. _Done when_ sluice protects `main` and the first change
   lands through it.

3. **L3 · The core and the Claude Code plugin.** `flume launch`,
   `status --json`, `tell`, `stop`, `retry`, `drop`, `tidy` and
   `prompt`, with places, the three kinds and a repository's or a
   launch's own. Prompt delivery is checked
   against the agent's transcript, and stage ends are read from
   deliverables, a change's from its OpenSpec files. The plugin brings
   `/launch` and serves `status` over MCP.

   _Needs_ L2. _Done when_ a fix launched from a session lands through
   sluice and its place is tidied, and a change runs construct, code and
   verify with its status showing its tasks ticked. A stage whose pane
   goes quiet with no deliverable is not ended, and tidying keeps a
   branch that holds commits its base lacks.

4. **L4 · The herdr plugin.** The sidebar of places in flight, with
   kind, stage, agent state and a change's tasks, and keys to open,
   tell, stop and retry.

   _Needs_ L3. _Done when_ the plugin installs from flume's tag, and a
   fix and a change launched from herdr show each stage in the sidebar
   until they land.

</Steps>

## Where it meets the others

<Steps>

1. **P6 · millpond sends an item through flume.** millpond's `send`
   names a repository and a kind, records the send, hands flume the
   brief, and writes the landing against the item from sluice's record.
   An inbox whose settings hand items to crew refuses a send.

   _Needs_ millpond's tools (P4) and L3. _Done when_ an item of two
   bumps sent from herdr lands through sluice, the item shows its
   landing, and a send from crew's inbox is refused.

2. **C7 · crew calls flume.** crew starts each stage's agent in a place
   through flume and reads stage ends from `flume status`, keeping its
   plans, bolts, slots, dispatcher, conductors and approvals.

   _Needs_ crew's amended record (C1) and L3. _Done when_ a bolt of one
   unit and one chore lands with every place and stage started through
   flume, and no crew code makes a worktree itself.

</Steps>

Others wait on flume too. headwater's plugins (H7) need L3 for the fix
key. sounding's core (Q3) needs L3 and its herdr plugin (Q4) needs L4,
since a sounding's place and session are flume's.
