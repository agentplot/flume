flume stands between what decides the work and what lands it. millpond,
headwater, sounding, crew and your own sessions decide what to launch;
flume makes the place and runs the stages; sluice lands the result.
Each tool stands alone, with its own Claude Code and herdr plugins.

## The family

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="millpond · the inbox">
    Sends an item to one worktree through flume, as a fix or a change.
    millpond records the send and hands flume the brief, and writes the
    landing against the item from sluice's record of the branch. An
    inbox crew keeps sends nothing to flume itself.
  </Card>
  <Card title="headwater · releases">
    Its fix key hands a release's brief to flume as a fix, and an agent
    makes the bump and submits it to sluice. In a repository crew runs,
    the key hands the release to millpond instead, and the bump comes
    as a chore from crew's plan.
  </Card>
  <Card title="sounding · questions">
    A question runs as flume's kind `question`. flume makes the place on
    `sounding/<name>` and runs the session; sounding keeps the question,
    its rounds and the conclusion you decide. A sounding waiting on you
    shows in flume's herdr sidebar.
  </Card>
  <Card title="sluice · landing">
    Lands what the last stage submits, tested as merged. flume never
    lands anything itself: it reads sluice's verdict and tidies the
    place once the work lands.
  </Card>
  <Card title="crew · teams and plans">
    Keeps everything that spans many places: plans, bolts, slots, the
    dispatcher, conductors, approvals, and conflicts between units. crew
    asks flume for each place and each stage's agent, as it asks sluice
    for each landing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Flywheel · the Method">
    The Method the family belongs to. crew becomes Flywheel, which is
    built later on the same parts as millpond.
  </Card>

## Who launches

| Caller | Launches |
|---|---|
| You | `/launch <brief>` in any session, or the herdr plugin's key |
| millpond | an item's send, as a fix or a change |
| headwater | the fix key, as a fix with the release's brief |
| sounding | a question, as the place and session of a sounding |
| crew | each unit, chore or fix of a team, as a place of a kind crew carries in the launch, every stage after the first waiting on crew's word |

Whoever launches, the place, the stages, prompt delivery and tidying
stay the same. Only the brief and who reads the result change.

## What flume never does

- **It never decides what to launch.** A caller names the repository,
  the kind, the brief, the name and the base.
- **It never plans across items.** crew's planner orders work today,
  and Flywheel's does later.
- **It never lands anything.** A stage submits to sluice, and sluice
  decides.
- **It never keeps an item's record.** millpond does. flume keeps only
  the live state of the places it runs.
- **It never removes what it did not make,** and never removes a branch
  that holds commits its base lacks.

## Built once, used everywhere

flume holds the one implementation of places, a fresh agent per stage,
prompt delivery, stages that end on their deliverable, and tidying. It
grows out of crew's rules for teams and plans (`teams.2` to `teams.4`,
`teams.11`, `teams.15`, and `plan.9`, `plan.10`, `plan.12` and
`plan.17`), and millpond, headwater, sounding and crew all build on it.
