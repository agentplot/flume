## What each document is

- **The capture design**, `design/capture/design.md`, covers every part
  that turns an outside event into landed work: headwater, eddy,
  millpond, flume, sounding, sluice, crew, Flywheel and the first fleet
  that runs them. Its section "Launching: flume" is flume's. `plan.md`
  beside it orders the work, and its decision 12 settles what flume is.
- **flume's statement**, `design/flume/requirements.md`, is written
  from that design and from the rules of crew's it takes over: a launch,
  places, kinds and stages, prompt delivery, stage ends, status,
  tidying, and what flume never does. A fresh model reconstructs flume
  from the statement alone, and the questions it asks are ruled and
  folded back in before the statement lands. It comes next, as plan
  step L1.
- **The design book** explains the design chapter by chapter, with the
  statement as its appendix.

## What binds the code

The statement binds. This repository cites its clauses by number and
never restates them. The rules a change here keeps, and the decisions
behind them, follow the statement into `docs/architecture/` and
`docs/adr/`. The founding decisions are three: one place, one agent per
stage; a stage ends on its deliverable; and plugins outside the core.

crew's rules for teams and plans, `teams.2` to `teams.4`, `teams.11`,
`teams.15`, and `plan.9`, `plan.10`, `plan.12` and `plan.17`, hold
today's version of what flume takes over. Once flume's statement lands,
crew amends them to name flume, and a question about places or stages
goes to flume's statement first.
