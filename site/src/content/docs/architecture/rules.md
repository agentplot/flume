A mirror of the rules in
[docs/architecture/core.md](https://github.com/boomdrivedev/flume/blob/main/docs/architecture/core.md),
which is the binding copy. The rules are written so a reviewer can point
at a diff and say which rule it breaks. The narrative is the statement
in boomdrivedev/blueprints, `design/flume/requirements.md`; a rule names
the clause it enforces and never restates it. The binding copy gives
each rule's details: what it rules out, what it leaves open, and its
sources. The [roadmap](/flume/roadmap/) names what is decided and not
yet built.

## Rules

### The core and its seams

- **[core.1]** Code outside a profile names no worktree tool, change tool, agent program, terminal, window program or merge queue.
- **[core.2]** Every seam of Part B is reached through exactly one interface, and a profile implements that interface and nothing of the core.
- **[core.3]** A repository's profiles are read from its `[profiles]` table and from nothing else; a seam the table leaves unbound is the no-tool profile, or unbound, and never guessed from another tool's files.
- **[core.4]** flume defines no field of the kind format: the format is weir's, and flume reads the part clause 23 names and ignores every section it does not know.
- **[core.34]** A kind from any source but flume's own, the repository's and a launch's runs only as a lock records it, its content's hash read at each launch and the launch refused where it differs; a kind's name and version resolve as clause 154 orders and never by a guess between sources; flume fetches nothing and writes no lock at a launch or a settle; and a kind refers to nothing outside its package but its landing fix, the kind a kind with no stages names for its fixes by that kind's identity with its version, and names nothing a host owns. (Amended 2026-11-07 by record 0056's amendment, settled by agentplot-design, and on 2026-11-08 to weir record 0005, accepted by the user on 2026-11-07 on their card.)
- **[core.29]** No call to herdr is made without a bound, and one that does not answer within it is read as unknown or a refusal in herdr's words; every command and field a profile reads of herdr is checked against the herdr the tree pins.

### Branches, submissions and landings

- **[core.5]** No code path commits, merges, rebases, pushes or lands in a place, and the only branch flume moves is a place's own, at its launch and at its tidy.
- **[core.6]** A place is submitted only by the settle that follows its last stage's delivery, through the merge-queue seam, to its base and to no other branch, with no wait held on it; its outcome is read from the merge queue's record by the settles that follow, never from an agent, and the merge queue removes nothing of the place.
- **[core.7]** A place's branch is cut from the base's current revision: where the merge queue's profile says the base lives, where a merge queue lands the base, and otherwise origin's after flume's own fetch, the host's own branch read only where the repository has no `origin` or origin holds no such branch; a launch that cannot read it is refused, and status says where it was read.
- **[core.8]** A launch is refused whole, leaving nothing it made, when anything of it already stands or its kind asks for more than flume runs.
- **[core.47]** A place is made ready only by its merge queue's equipping of it, asked at the revision its launch read; flume runs no readiness step of its own, reads no tool's step from the merge queue's policy, and keeps nothing a step makes.
- **[core.53]** A branch other places land on, and that itself lands, is a base place's, a place whose kind lands and has no stages, known by that shape alone: it has no worktree and is its branch and its record; the merge queue alone makes and removes its branch, at flume's asking; it runs no agent; it is submitted only on its caller's retry, by its branch's name from the repository, once no place on its branch is live, its outcome read from the merge queue's record of its branch; while it waits on its caller with no place on its branch live, each settle forecasts its branch against its base through the merge queue, and a conflict so forecast with the base, or a failed or conflicting submission, launches the fix its kind names on its branch, one at a time, each forecast and submission acted on once, within its kind's bound; and flume names no depth and no use of one. (Amended 2026-11-07 by record 0056, accepted by the user the same day on their card, and its amendment, settled by agentplot-design.)

### Stages and agents

- **[core.9]** A stage's end is read from its deliverable at one head of the place's branch, or from a stop-short carrying the start's own secret; no code reads an end from a terminal's state or an agent's exit.
- **[core.10]** Each start of a stage, each repetition of a repeating stage among them, starts exactly one fresh agent, a place has at most one agent at any moment, every agent carries an identity no other live agent on the host carries, an agent is its stage's only on its own word, or, where its profile reads no word of its program's, its launcher's, that it started under that start's identity and the settings it recorded, its mode among them where they give one and its profile reads it back, an agent the terminal brought back bare in a conversation's pane is ended only by its caller's stop, drop or done, a start that carries a conversation opens only while no process runs that conversation, and no agent that is not quiet is ended unless a caller forced it.
- **[core.11]** A prompt or a tell is sent only to a waiting agent that has said it started, any input it holds typed cleared first, read back as received within 60 seconds or sent once more, and never left typed for a hand; a notice is sent only to a recipient that asks no question, and counts handed over on the terminal's answer, delivering nothing by itself. (Amended 2026-11-08 by record 0061, proposed.)
- **[core.12]** flume answers on an agent's behalf only the question whether it may work in the place, and, in a stage that delivers a plan, declines the agent's ask to leave its planning mode; it never answers one that widens what an agent may do, and never accepts a plan.
- **[core.30]** An agent's settings are resolved at its start from the layers clause 32 orders and from nothing else, the host's configuration as it stands then and every other layer as the launch read it, each setting taken whole; its mode is one of those settings, one of flume's four or a program's own value under that program's name, which its profile maps to exactly that setting or refuses, but a plan stage's, which is `plan`, and none is given where no layer gives one, the account's own configuration deciding; its model is its program's own name, passed as written; its effort is its program's own word, taken only where its profile names it among its program's words and passed as that profile takes it, and none is given where no layer gives one; where no layer names its program and it has a pool, its program is its account's; its environment is the login environment with its account's variables over it, and nothing else gives one; and status names where each setting and each variable came from. (Amended 2026-11-05 by record 0044, accepted by the user on 2026-11-06 on their card.)
- **[core.31]** A repetition's prompt differs from its stage's other repetitions' only after every part they share: flume adds nothing between a prompt's parts, and flume's own repeating prompts put the group, the notes' path and the words last; flume names a stage's notes by their path and never reads, writes or carries what they hold.
- **[core.32]** An account is chosen at each start of an agent whose stream or kind has a pool, under the room lock: for the retry that carries a lost conversation, the account its lost start ran under while that account is usable, then the intent's affinity for the stage's program while that account is usable, else the pool's strategy; an account past its pool's threshold by its reading takes no new start, under any strategy, and that reading is the usage windows its agent program last reported for it to flume in a session flume started, kept in the host's own documents, asking nothing of the program or its maker and reading no file a host writes for it; a stage whose pool holds no account that can run it is refused, at its launch or its start, and never left waiting; a spent account is recorded in the host's state until its reset and skipped by every flume process, its agent's stage started again on the account so chosen with no caller's word and no going-back counted, carrying that agent's own conversation where its program can carry it there and afresh otherwise; a caller's retry of a conversation reported lost carries its agent's conversation so, and no other retry carries one; flume copies, links and writes nothing in an account's configuration home; and every move of a stage or an intent between accounts is said in status with why, an intent's for which program, and whether a conversation went with it. (Amended 2026-11-02 by record 0020's two amendments, accepted by the user the same day, the first on their review and the second on their card; amended 2026-11-05 by record 0035, accepted by the user the same day; amended 2026-11-06 by record 0052, accepted by the user the same day on their card; amended 2026-11-09 by record 0062, accepted by the user that day in agentplot-design's pane.)
- **[core.33]** An agent's standing prompt is the goals, the intent's brief, the stage's standing instructions and its settings' instructions, in that order, each as given and joined by one fixed separator; nothing that differs between starts is in the first three, no part is filled in them, and its prompt holds the unit's brief and facts and then its run's; a kind gives only the standing instructions, and the goals and the briefs come from the caller or the repository.
- **[core.37]** A stage that delivers a plan starts in its program's planning mode, ends only on its caller's finish once a plan was put for review, and keeps its agent live while it is for review, so a tell reaches the same session; the finish accepts the newest plan, kept as it stood in the place's own folder for the stages after it.
- **[core.39]** Every program a terminal starts runs under an agent profile, its own or the common one; each part of the agent seam is built for the program from its own record or degraded as its profile declares, never read from the terminal where its profile builds it and never otherwise than clause 171 says, and status names each degraded part with the agent; a profile's entry for its program is flume's own code, checked against the release flume pins; and a mode, a model, an effort or a plan its profile cannot honour refuses the launch, or the stage at a later start, naming the program. (Amended 2026-11-05 by record 0044, accepted by the user on 2026-11-06 on their card.)
- **[core.44]** Where the launch's settings and the stage's declaration name no place in the terminal, a stage's agent runs in its place's stream's session: the one the stream names for the place's kind's stage, else for its kind, else the one the host's configuration names for the stream, else the one named by the stream's name, and, for the host's default stream naming none, flume's own; only the launch's settings and the stream's own table name a session, a session a stream names for a kind or a stage is that stream's alone, each is read at each start, an agent stays where it opened, and status names each agent's session with where it came from. (Amended 2026-11-06 by record 0048 and its amendment, each accepted by the user the same day, the amendment on their card.)
- **[core.49]** A stage's agent opens in its stream's terminal, the one the host's configuration names for the stream, else the host's, read at each start and recorded with it; every later call on that agent goes to the terminal its start recorded and to no other, and a place is tidied in each terminal its starts recorded; and a stream whose terminal flume carries no profile for refuses its launches, its starts and its views, naming it, and nothing else.
- **[core.50]** A program's own settings are given under its name and reach no other program, by a layer's own keys and a file it names, the file's beneath its own; each top-level key is taken whole from the first layer that gives it and passed as written, the way its profile takes them, never the named file as it stands, never translated, merged inside or read by flume; a key its profile reserves for another setting or the account is refused where a layer writes it and left out of a named file, status naming it; a value shaped as a credential is refused before anything holds it; and where no layer gives one, nothing is passed.

### Settling, surfaces and state

- **[core.13]** flume starts no process that outlives a command of its own, and no command of flume's waits on the merge queue: every settle runs inside a command, started by an agent going quiet, by a caller keeping watch over the host or by any other caller, never by a terminal or a plugin of one, and whether flume runs attended is read from the host's watch alone.
- **[core.14]** Every surface answers from one core through one form a program reads, which only ever gains fields, and no surface computes a stage's end, a status, a submission or a tidy of its own.
- **[core.48]** A stream's view is opened only at a caller's asking for one of the stream's sessions by the session's name, never for a stream, and never at a launch, a settle or a start, in the view the host's configuration names for the stream, else the host's: in a window, through the window seam alone, running nothing but the stream's terminal's attach for that session, the stream's own as a start resolves it or one the stream names for a kind or a stage, under the title the host's configuration declares for the stream or else its name for the stream's own, and that session's name for another; or in the terminal's own app, through its attach alone; flume keeps, reads, finds again and closes nothing of a window, and neither the window nor the view in it is a process of flume's. (Amended 2026-11-06 by record 0048 and its amendment, each accepted by the user the same day, the amendment on their card.)
- **[core.15]** Status reads every fact of a deliverable, a change or a submission where it lives when asked, and flume keeps no record of one between asks.
- **[core.52]** flume's list is read from its held state, each live agent's own record and each stream's terminal once, by the terminal's runs alone: no place's worktree, branch, deliverable, change or submission, and no agent's state in its terminal; it gives a place's state in the words status gives it and none of a place's long words, and settles, starts, sends and writes nothing.
- **[core.16]** A place's live state is one document on the host, written by compare-and-set on its version, gone when the place is tidied, and nothing of flume's is written into a repository's content.
- **[core.17]** Every act is safe to repeat, and no act leaves a worktree or a branch of flume's that its held state does not name.
- **[core.18]** flume removes only places it made, through the repository's teardown, never a place holding uncommitted or unignored work or a lock, or one in which a process runs its conversation's session, and never a branch holding a commit whose change its base lacks.
- **[core.23]** What a stage made is read from the line the stage's run wrote and carried as written, a durable thing by its address and a thing that must run by the branch and revision that bring it up; flume builds, checks or rewrites no address of its own, a stage starts no server that outlives its place, and a declared thing with no line is a deliverable not yet made.
- **[core.24]** A place waits on its caller only as status says, which move and since when, a hold carrying its caller's words whether put at the launch or after it, on a stage, its submission or the move the place waits on; a place waiting under no hold's words for five minutes, or the launch's bound, is said stalled and delivered that once to each recipient of it, to the role the stop-short it waits on named and to the answerer behind its stream; flume watches no event for a hold and ends none itself, but a hold on the waiting move lapses at its bound. (Amended 2026-11-06 by record 0039, its design approved by the user the same day.)
- **[core.25]** A place's end is one of weir's deliveries, recorded once inside the settle that reads it with each of its recipients, and delivered to a recipient only once that recipient confirms it by its key, by `flume confirm` or on receipt in the call that hands it over; a notice through the terminal into the recipient's own pane, a wait, the inbox and a session's start each hand it over and are recorded so, to whom, when and by what, and none of them, a timeout or an expiry delivers it; a notice goes to a recipient once, while nothing has handed the end to it; a wait is by place, answers a pending end at once and otherwise follows the host's state under a timeout, so a caller whose session restarted loses nothing; a recipient collects what is pending for it by `flume inbox`, handed again at each until it confirms it, and a read confirms nothing; an end pending past the host's span shows expired with its recipient, and a name never confirmed under shows as such; a recipient no pane holds is sent no notice, its ends pending until it collects them by `flume inbox` or a wait as itself; and a drop or a done is an end sent by no notice and waking no role, pending for each recipient of the place that is no role's address. (Amended 2026-11-08 by record 0060, accepted by the user that day, and by record 0061, proposed.)
- **[core.26]** A stage starts only while the host's agents running, every caller's counted together, are under the bound the host's configuration states, its stream's are under the stream's less what the stream's other labels hold `reserved` and do not use, an account of its pool is usable, and, for a place's first start, its stream's points bound and pace leave room for its points, and otherwise waits for room; a settle starts the waiting stages by their streams' priority, then, within a stream, a label's below what it is `owed` first, then their places', as many as there is room for, passing over one whose stream or pool has no room, and ends no agent for one; a place's priority is its caller's, given at the launch and changed after, a stream's and what a label is owed the host's, and flume orders places by nothing else; every launch is taken and none refused for want of room; status says the agents running, the bound, the room and the queue; a host that states no bound of its own and no stream's bound has no queue. (Amended 2026-11-03 by record 0033, accepted 2026-11-07; its keys renamed 2026-11-09 by record 0063, accepted by the user that day in agentplot-design's pane.)
- **[core.28]** A recipient may be a role's address, `<stream>/<role>`, a role of a stream the host declares, declared by a source of the host and never by the host's configuration; an end pending for an address that was never handed to it, with no run live or waiting, or a carrier's wake, launches the kind its source's entry names for the address, at most one run of it at once, named per address, in its address's stream, with its entry's settings and intent, declared done at its delivery with no caller's word or, where its kind lands, submitted there as any place is, and its stall delivered to the answerer behind its stream and never to its own address; flume decides nothing of what the run does; an address its source gives a standing agent instead has its ends delivered into that agent and wakes no run, and a command that names no recipient, run in that agent's pane, acts as that address; and a launch naming an address that wakes nothing and is not carried is refused only where no answerer stands behind its stream. (Amended 2026-11-06 by record 0039, its design approved by the user the same day, and 2026-11-08 by record 0061, proposed; and 2026-11-14 by record 0013's amendment of that day.)
- **[core.27]** A kind runs through on its own: a stage's delivery starts the next stage and the last stage's delivery submits the place, with no caller's word unless the launch or a hold asks for one; a failed or conflicting submission, and a stop-short that found work from the kind's last stage where the stage the kind names for a failed submission is before it, start that stage with the merge queue's or the agent's words, counted together toward the kind's bound; a stop-short says whether it needs an answer or found work, every agent told that a found is work a stage before its own must do again, and status says which with the words; found work from any other stage, and from a last stage that is its kind's fix round, is taken as needing an answer; an agent that stops short needing an answer is kept open for it, where its terminal hands a running agent words, for the time the launch's or the host's settings give, one hour where neither gives one; an answer that comes while it is kept open is handed to its session and recorded on the stop-short it answers, and the stage carries on there under the same start; any other answer is given only by a retry whose prompt carries the question with the answer; and flume ends an asking agent once it is no longer kept open and is quiet. (Amended 2026-11-06 by record 0011's amendment, the design agent's.)
- **[core.36]** flume reads no base's checks and launches no place of its own on a base's failure: a fix of a base whose own checks failed after a landing is a fix place a caller launched on that base with the failure's words as its brief, cut from the base's current revision and submitted to it through the merge queue, its submission held where the base's landings wait on a word the merge queue's policy does not ask, and nothing else moved for it.
- **[core.38]** A place's points are its caller's, a number of the Fibonacci scale fixed at the launch, which flume never estimates, changes or counts by a guess; they measure its agents' work, in flight from its first start until its last stage delivers and again through a fix round; a stream's points bound and its pace hold first starts alone, the pace over the rolling span that ends at each start and charged in the host's own documents; and machine time and tokens are reported beside the points and never limited.
- **[core.46]** A place's label is the one the host's configuration gives its repository's kind, else its kind, else its kind's own, else none, read at each start, and flume gives it no meaning but its share of its stream's agents: within a stream, a stage of a label running fewer agents than it is `owed` starts before every stage of a label running what it is owed or more, whatever their priorities; a stage starts only while its stream's room passes what the stream's other labels hold `reserved` and do not use; no agent is ended for what a label is owed; a label has no pool, account, points bound or pace of its own; a stream whose labels are owed more than its bound together, or whose label holds more `reserved` than it is `owed` or any key but `owed` and `reserved`, starts no stage, each refused naming why (record 0063); and status gives each place's label with where it came from and each stream's labels.
- **[core.45]** The host's configuration is its managed `config.toml` and its local `config.local.toml` read over it key by key at each reading, and flume writes neither; the local layer sets only the keys C.7 lists and no key the managed layer pins; a local layer that sets any other key, or cannot be read, is not taken at all, the managed layer read alone and status naming why; and status gives every value with the layer it came from.
- **[core.54]** Every place but a role's run has a recipient its ends reach, a role's run woken for them, a standing agent or a caller that collects them; a stop-short may name the role it is for, its own stream's by the role alone and any other stream's by its address; the answerer behind a place's stream is delivered every stall besides the place's recipients, but its own run's, which reaches no one; and an answer naming a stop-short's key is taken only while the place waits on that stop-short. (Drawn 2026-11-06 from record 0039, its design approved by the user the same day.)

### Design and records

- **[core.19]** A change that bears on a clause cites the clause by number; a clause that no longer holds is changed in the statement first, never worked around here.
- **[core.20]** Nothing commercial, no pricing, tier or customer, appears in this repository.
- **[core.35]** The site, the README, the plugins' commands and the commands' help and output speak to any host's operator: they name no tool, team, host or word of ours as the reader's own, and name a tool only as a product flume integrates with.

### The gate

- **[core.21]** Every file the tree tracks is owned by a formatter the gate runs, or is excluded by name with its reason; every crate, every directory under `plugins/`, and `site/`, is a moon project of its own, listed in `.moon/workspace.yml` with its language declared, whose check-fast tasks validate, lint and test it, one task per check.
- **[core.22]** Every tool a gate task runs is the version the tree pins, on every host that runs the gate.
- **[core.51]** A check finds room for what it makes, a socket's path among them, in the scratch the merge queue gives its place, and a test that finds none fails naming what lacked room; it never passes, skips or returns early without running.
