## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A place whose conversation a process runs is kept

A tidy SHALL keep a place whose kind holds a conversation while the
agent profile's live answers that a process runs the conversation of
its last conversation start, or cannot read whether one does, naming
each process as `kept while its session runs`; the next tidy after the
process ends SHALL remove it as any place (64, 207; core.18).

#### Scenario: A dropped place whose session a person runs

- **WHEN** a place whose talk is lost is dropped while its session is scripted live, then the session ends and the place is tidied again
- **THEN** the first tidy keeps it naming the process, and the fake place records a remove only at the second (64, 207, S117)
