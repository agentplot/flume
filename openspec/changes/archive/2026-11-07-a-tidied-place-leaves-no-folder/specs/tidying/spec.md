## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A place read gone is removed all the same

A tidy SHALL hand a place whose work is over and that the place seam
reads gone to the place seam's remove, as it does a place read live and
holding nothing, so nothing of it stands at its location; a remove it
refuses SHALL keep the place, named with its step, for the next tidy
(62, 66, 81; record 0057).

#### Scenario: A place read gone

- **WHEN** a place whose worktree the fake place no longer holds is declared done while the fake refuses its remove, then the refusal is mended and the place tidied again
- **THEN** the first tidy keeps it naming the step after one remove, and the second removes it after a second remove
