## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: A place read gone is removed all the same

A tidy SHALL hand a place whose work is over and that the place seam
reads gone to the place seam's remove, as it does a place read live and
holding nothing, so nothing of it stands at its location; a remove it
refuses SHALL keep the place in its held state, named with its step,
and each later settle SHALL tidy it again, with no caller's word, until
its remove passes (62, 66, 76, 81; record 0057).

#### Scenario: A place read gone

- **WHEN** a place whose worktree the fake place no longer holds is declared done while the fake refuses its remove, then the refusal is mended and the place settled
- **THEN** the done keeps it naming the step after one remove, and the settle removes it after a second remove, with no tidy asked (76; `a_place_read_gone` in `crates/flume-conformance/src/engine/tidying.rs`)
